# Design — Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image

One-page newspaper front page, 1024×1024, cream paper, black serif headlines,
maroon + navy accents, flat original vector drawings (no photos).

## Structure (winner-template pattern)

- Kicker: "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY" + "35 STORIES · 7 REGIONS · EVERY ONE DATED TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026"
- Masthead: "Tuesday 24 November 2026" + one-line sub-headline of the 3 leads
- Stat strip (6): +31% Nvidia, +6.5% Nasdaq, 24 dead Indonesia fire, $44bn cyber deal,
  $952bn Nvidia one-day gain, 14 games chess match
- Region bar: Europe 8 · Asia 8 · Middle East 6 · Americas 7 · Africa 3 · Global 2 · Oceania 1 (= 35)
- 3 lead stories: (1) Nvidia — Huang reinstated, shares +31% (Reuters/CNN);
  (2) Ukraine sinks Russian warship off Libya (Reuters);
  (3) NATO sends AWACS to Poland (Reuters)
- 10 small stories: South Korea airspace alert; Gaza strikes + settler arson;
  Indonesia factory fire; Iran v US blockade; Mexico "Chapo Isidro" arrest;
  Modi AI centre IIT Patna; Gukesh v Sindarov Game 1; CrowdStrike–Zscaler $44bn;
  UK hosts 2035 Women's World Cup; Germany adopts Kyiv's Delta tool
- Map "WHERE IT HAPPENED" with numbered pins 1–13
- "ALSO ON TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER — 22 MORE STORIES" (items 14–35), one line each, source per item
- DATE CHECK box: COP31 collapse (22 Nov), Alcaraz ATP Finals title (22 Nov),
  BepiColombo Mercury orbit (22 Nov), Fiona the hippo's death (19 Nov),
  Huang's resignation (11 days earlier) — all deliberately excluded
- Footer: every story happened, or was first reported, on Tuesday 24 November 2026
